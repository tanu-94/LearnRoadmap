Hospital Database Relational Schema

This 3-table relational schema powers all SQL, Python, and Power BI lessons:

departments Dimension
  • dept_id (PK)
  • dept_name
staff Core Entity
  • staff_id (PK)
  • staff_name
  • role
  • dept_id (FK)
  • salary
  • join_date
shifts Fact (Activity)
  • shift_id (PK)
  • staff_id (FK)
  • shift_date
  • shift_type
  • hours_worked

Relational Cardinality:

• departments.dept_id (1) ────< staff.dept_id (N)

• staff.staff_id (1) ────────< shifts.staff_id (N)
